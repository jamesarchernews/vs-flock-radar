> LEGAL & ETHICAL PROTOCOLS

This application is an investigative journalism data-visualization tool developed for The Valley Star.

  • PUBLIC DATA: Utilizes crowdsourced, publicly accessible Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT).
  • NO BACKDOOR ACCESS: This system does not interface with, query, or compromise proprietary law enforcement or corporate databases.
  • FIRST AMENDMENT: Aggregation and visualization of public infrastructure in plain view is protected news-gathering activity.

> METHODOLOGY

Nodes are extracted via OpenStreetMap (OSM) APIs using spatial grid-chunking. Due to the crowdsourced nature of OSINT, real-time operational status of individual hardware nodes cannot be independently verified.
